# Model photography — credits and licences

One photograph per vehicle model, shown on the job card, the customer
page, the search results and the model picker. All are normalised to the
same frame so nothing jumps between a model that has its own photograph
and one falling back to `../bodies/`.

## Wikimedia Commons

Commons. **Most carry a CC BY or CC BY-SA licence, which makes
attribution a condition of use, not a courtesy.** The table below is that
attribution; it has to stay reachable from the shipped app — an "Image
credits" entry in the account sheet is enough.

Sourced with `harvest_search.py` / `harvest_fetch.py`. Non-commercial and
no-derivatives files were rejected at search time: neither can ship in a
product.
